
# ABILITY LIBRARY

This file defines the base ability set used by cards.

Abilities may appear on:
- Mages
- Beasts
- Relics
- Totems

-----------------------------------

OFFENSIVE ABILITIES

Fireball
Deal 6 Fire damage.

Lightning Bolt
Deal 7 Lightning damage.

Ice Lance
Deal 5 Ice damage and reduce Speed by 2.

Arcane Blast
Deal 8 Arcane damage.

Shadow Strike
Deal 6 damage and apply Curse.

Cleave
Attack two enemies.

-----------------------------------

DEFENSIVE ABILITIES

Barrier
Gain +4 Defense for one turn.

Stone Skin
Gain +3 Defense permanently.

Reflect
Reflect next spell.

Shield Wall
All allies gain +2 Defense.

-----------------------------------

HEALING ABILITIES

Heal
Restore 6 HP.

Greater Heal
Restore 10 HP.

Nature Regrowth
Restore 4 HP per turn for 3 turns.

Purify
Remove negative effects.

-----------------------------------

CHAOS ABILITIES

Chaos Swap
Swap Attack and Defense.

Random Spell
Cast random ability.

Mirror Trick
Copy enemy ability.

Stat Flip
Invert stats temporarily.

-----------------------------------

BEAST ABILITIES

Pack Tactics
Beasts gain +2 Attack.

Primal Roar
Enemies lose 2 Defense.

Summon Cub
Summon L1 beast.

-----------------------------------

TOTEM EFFECTS

Storm Totem
Lightning units gain +2 Attack.

Forest Totem
Nature units gain +3 HP.

Shadow Totem
Shadow spells cost 1 less.
