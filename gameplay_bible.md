
# THE WARDED ONES TCG – GAMEPLAY BIBLE

This document defines core gameplay rules.

-----------------------------------

WIN CONDITION

Reduce opponent Life to zero.

Starting Life: 30

-----------------------------------

RESOURCE SYSTEM

Automatic resource growth.

Turn 1 = 1 resource
Turn 2 = 2 resources
Turn 3 = 3 resources

Maximum = 10

-----------------------------------

BATTLEFIELD STRUCTURE

Each player may control:

1 Mage
4 Fighters
3 Beasts
2 Totems

Relics attach to units.

-----------------------------------

EVOLUTION SYSTEM

Cards evolve via level cards.

L1 → L2 → L3

Rules
L2 requires L1 in play
L3 requires L2

Equipment remains attached.

-----------------------------------

COMBAT SYSTEM

Damage formula

Damage = Attack - Defense

Minimum damage = 1

-----------------------------------

TURN STRUCTURE

1 Draw Phase
Draw a card

2 Resource Phase
Increase resource

3 Main Phase
Play cards

4 Combat Phase
Declare attacks

5 End Phase
Resolve effects

-----------------------------------

DECK SIZE

Recommended 40 cards.

Suggested composition

1 Mage
6 Fighters
10 Beasts
10 Relics
5 Totems
8 Utility cards
